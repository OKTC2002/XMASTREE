Three Christmas Eve Secrets to Make Sure Santa Finds Your House!
1. The Christmas Eve Window Watch: Legend has it that Santa looks for houses with a little Christmas sparkle. Before bedtime, turn on the Christmas tree lights, light up a window, and make sure the house looks merry and bright. A dark house might make him think everyone has gone on vacation! Nobody wants Santa leaving a present on the porch.

2. The Official Santa Landing Strip: If you don't have a chimney, don't panic! Some families hang a special magic key outside the front door on Christmas Eve. The key is said to work ONLY for Santa. (Important: Regular house keys will not do. Santa has very particular security standards!)

3. The Silent Night Test: Here's an old-fashioned bit of Christmas wisdom: Santa only visits when everyone is fast asleep. Some families even say you must be dreaming of Christmas before he can land. So close those eyes, stop listening for sleigh bells, and whatever you do, don't peek! If you hear a jingle in the night, that's your sign he's getting close.

Remember: Christmas magic works best when everyone is tucked in, the lights are twinkling, and the grown-ups are very good at keeping secrets!
